Privacy Policy

Effective September 17, 2026

CafCast is a free iPhone app for rating dishes at Stav Hall while a meal is being served. It is an independent project. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and how to delete it.

The short version

  • There is no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, or password.
  • We store an anonymous account ID, your votes, a record of when the app is opened, the IP address your sign-in session connects from, and, only if you turn on notifications, a push token.
  • No ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Anonymous account ID. The first time you open CafCast, our backend creates a random account ID for you. It is not connected to your name, email, or Apple ID. The app keeps its sign-in in your iPhone's Keychain, so the same ID usually comes back if you delete and reinstall the app.
  • Your votes. For each dish you rate: the dish, your rating (Loved it, Fine, or Nope), any mood tags you pick from a fixed list, and when you voted. Votes are stored with your account ID so you get one vote per dish and can change or remove it. Other people only ever see totals, never who voted what. We also note which account cast the first vote on a dish, so the app can show that person a "first" badge.
  • Push token (optional). If you tap "Notify me" and allow notifications, Apple gives the app a device token, which we store with your account ID so we can send you one notification when each meal opens.
  • App usage. To see whether CafCast is useful, we record when the app is opened, which screen it opened on, whether notifications are allowed, whether you tapped a meal notification, and whether you chose to look at the board without voting. These are counts of what happens in the app, stored with your account ID. We do not record anything you type, and there is no third-party analytics service in the app.
  • Connection data. When the app talks to our backend, we record your IP address and basic device and app information with your sign-in session. This is used to keep the service running and secure. We do not use it to find your location.

On your phone, the app also saves a few settings, such as whether you have seen the welcome screen and which meals you rated today. These stay on your device.

What we do not collect

No name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, advertising identifier, or browsing history. CafCast contains no advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To show the live board and each dish's verdict.
  • To keep it to one vote per dish per account and let you change or remove your vote.
  • To remember that you already voted today, including after a reinstall.
  • To send meal notifications, if you turned them on.
  • To prevent abuse and keep the service working.
  • To count how many students use the app, how many vote, and whether notifications help, so we can improve it.

We read these numbers ourselves, as totals on a private dashboard. We do not send your data to any advertising or analytics company.

Who else receives data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service on servers in the United States and processes this data only to run CafCast for us.
  • Apple Push Notification service receives your push token and the notification text to deliver meal notifications.

These providers are required to protect the data at least as well as this policy describes. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Your account ID, votes, app usage records, and push token are kept until you delete your data. Votes on past meals are kept so dish scores stay accurate over time. Connection data is stored in our database with your sign-in session and is deleted when you delete your data. Our hosting provider also keeps short-term request logs as part of running the service. A push token is also removed when Apple tells us it is no longer valid, for example after the app is deleted.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: in the app, tap the ⓘ next to the CafCast name, then Delete my data. This permanently deletes your account ID, every vote and mood tag you have cast (including past meals), your "first" badges, and your push token. The app then starts over as a new anonymous voter. This does not turn notifications off: if notifications are still allowed for CafCast in the iPhone Settings app, the app registers a new push token for the new account and meal notifications keep coming.
  • Remove a single vote: open the dish during the meal and tap "Remove my vote".
  • Stop notifications: turn them off for CafCast in the iPhone Settings app. Deleting your data does not stop them on its own, because the app signs in as a new anonymous voter and registers a new push token.
  • Questions or help deleting data: email cafcast.app@gmail.com.

Children

CafCast is meant for college students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, email us and we will delete the data.

Not affiliated

CafCast is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by St. Olaf College or Bon Appétit Management Company. Menus and hours come from Bon Appétit's public Stav Hall menu.

How CafCast works

While a meal is being served at Stav Hall, tap a dish you ate and pick Loved it, Fine, or Nope. Rating one dish opens the full live board for the rest of the day. Each dish gets a verdict from everyone's votes: Worth it, Fine, or Skip.

Common questions

Why can't I vote right now?
Voting is only open while a meal is being served, so scores come from people eating that meal. Between meals the app shows when the next meal opens and what is on the menu.

The app says today's menu isn't up yet.
We read the menu from Bon Appétit's public Stav Hall page. If it hasn't posted yet, tap "Check again" in a little while.

How do I get notified when a meal opens?
Between meals, tap "Notify me" and allow notifications. To stop them, turn off notifications for CafCast in the iPhone Settings app.

How do I delete my data?
Tap the ⓘ next to the CafCast name, then Delete my data. See the Privacy Policy for what is deleted.

Do I need an account?
No. The app creates an anonymous account automatically. There is no sign-up and no password.
